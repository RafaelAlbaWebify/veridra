## 2. Current public-site evidence (rechecked 2026-10-13)

### Finding CD-01 — Conflicting patient-facing opening hours

On the **same public contact page** (https://www.crowndental.ie/contact-us/), the business-specific opening-hours block shows:

- Monday 08:30–18:30; Tuesday 10:00–19:00; Wednesday 09:00–18:30;
- Thursday 10:00–18:30; Friday 10:00–17:30; Saturday 10:00–17:30.

The **footer opening-hours block on that page** displays a different schedule:

- Monday–Friday 09:30–18:30; Saturday 09:30–18:00.

Further pages reuse the 09:30–18:30 / Saturday 09:30–18:00 footer (https://www.crowndental.ie/about-us/). Some pages display the weekday-varying schedule as well (https://www.crowndental.ie/department/dentist/).

**Illustrative contradiction, Saturday:** 10:00–17:30 versus 09:30–18:00.

**Confidence:** HIGH that contradictory schedules are publicly displayed on the inspected pages.  
**Unknown:** the actual, authoritative opening hours; whether individual service or department hours legitimately differ; how many patients see or act on each block.

**Careful owner-facing wording:** “Your website currently displays different opening hours on the contact page and its footer. Confirming the correct schedule and aligning these displays could reduce confusion for patients arranging appointments.”

**Never claim:** lost appointments, measurable conversion loss, Google ranking impact, wrong opening hours, or guaranteed financial uplift without evidence.

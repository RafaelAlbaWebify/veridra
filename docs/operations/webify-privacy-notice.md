## 1. Purpose

This notice explains how WEBIFY LIMITED ("Webify", "we", "us") processes personal data when acting as a controller in relation to:

- business prospects and their representatives;
- customer and prospective-customer contacts;
- suppliers and business partners;
- billing and contract contacts;
- people who communicate with Webify about its services.

Where Webify processes personal data solely on a customer's documented instructions as a processor, the Webify Data Processing Addendum and the customer's own privacy information govern that processing instead.

## 2. Personal data we may process

Depending on the relationship, Webify may process:

- name, role/title and employer/business;
- professional email address, business telephone number and business address;
- public business-profile or website information;
- correspondence, meeting notes, discovery requirements and relationship history;
- proposal, contract, order, approval and support metadata;
- invoice, payment-status, tax/VAT and provider reference information;
- technical contact, authorized-access and project metadata;
- public digital-presence evidence relevant to an assessment.

Webify does not intentionally collect passwords, MFA codes, payment-card data, patient/clinical information or other special-category/highly sensitive personal data under the standard Presence Care workflow.

## 3. Sources

We may receive personal data:

- directly from you or your organisation;
- from communications with Webify;
- from publicly accessible business websites, company pages, professional directories, Google Business Profiles and similar business-facing sources;
- from an authorised colleague or representative of your organisation;
- from approved service providers used for contracting, billing or communications.

When prospect data is obtained from a public or third-party source rather than directly from the individual, Webify will provide the information required by GDPR Article 14 no later than the first communication where that communication is used to contact the individual.

## 4. Purposes and lawful bases

### Business enquiries, discovery, proposals and pre-contract steps
Purpose: respond to enquiries, understand requirements, prepare proposals and manage a prospective business relationship.

Lawful basis:
- steps requested before entering a contract where applicable; and/or
- Webify's legitimate interests in operating and developing its B2B professional-services business.

### B2B prospecting and direct marketing
Purpose: identify and contact organisations that may reasonably benefit from Webify services.

Lawful basis for GDPR processing: Webify's legitimate interests in proportionate B2B business development, subject to the recipient's rights and the separate Irish ePrivacy rules governing the communication itself.

Webify does not treat legitimate interests as permission to send any electronic marketing message. Each outreach communication must separately pass the Webify B2B Outreach & Direct Marketing Compliance SOP.

You may object to processing for direct marketing at any time. Once you object, Webify will stop using your personal data for direct marketing and may retain a minimal suppression record solely to ensure the objection is respected.

### Customer service delivery and relationship management
Purpose: administer contracts, authorised access, projects, reporting, support, service changes and offboarding.

Lawful basis:
- performance of a contract or steps connected with it where the individual is a contracting party; and/or
- Webify's legitimate interests in administering a B2B contract and communicating with the customer's authorised personnel.

### Billing, tax and accounting
Purpose: invoicing, payment reconciliation, tax records, fraud prevention and statutory accounting.

Lawful basis:
- legal obligations applicable to Webify;
- performance of the commercial relationship where applicable; and
- legitimate interests in receiving and reconciling payment.

### Security and legal claims
Purpose: protect systems, investigate incidents, preserve evidence and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Lawful basis:
- legitimate interests in security and legal protection; and/or
- legal obligation where applicable.

## 5. Direct-marketing rules

Webify's standard outreach model is B2B only.

For Ireland:

- a generic/corporate business email may be used for relevant B2B marketing unless the business has notified Webify that it does not consent;
- an email address identifying a natural person may be used without prior consent only where it reasonably appears to be used mainly in that person's commercial or official activity **and** the communication relates solely to that activity;
- personal/private-looking addresses must not be used for unsolicited marketing unless another lawful ePrivacy basis applies;
- each marketing email must identify Webify, provide a valid reply/contact address and include an easy way to object to further marketing;
- objections and opt-outs are effective immediately for future campaigns.

Other jurisdictions may impose stricter rules and must be checked before outreach outside the approved market.

## 6. Recipients and service providers

Personal data may be shared only where necessary with:

- Webify personnel/operators who need it;
- Stripe for approved billing/payment workflows;
- approved document, communications or infrastructure providers listed in the current Webify Subprocessor Register where they actually process personal data;
- professional advisers, authorities or regulators where required or reasonably necessary.

Webify does not sell personal data.

Real customer personal data must not be placed in an external service that is marked unapproved or unverified for that use in the Webify Subprocessor Register.

## 7. International transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, Webify will use an applicable lawful transfer mechanism such as an adequacy decision, the European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses, or another mechanism permitted by data-protection law.

The applicable provider/transfer decision is recorded through the Webify Subprocessor Register and transfer-assessment process.

## 8. Retention

Webify applies data minimisation and storage limitation.

Default operating periods:

- unconverted prospect/outreach records: up to 12 months after the last relevant interaction, unless there is a documented reason to retain them longer;
- direct-marketing suppression records: minimal information for as long as reasonably necessary to honour the objection;
- customer operational/project records: for the service term and a proportionate post-service period needed for support, disputes, security, continuity or legal claims;
- contracts, invoices and records required for Irish tax/accounting: normally at least six years where required by applicable Revenue record-keeping rules;
- security/incident evidence: according to the seriousness of the incident and applicable legal requirements.

Where a shorter or longer period is required by law, contract or a documented legal claim, that requirement prevails.

## 9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

- access your personal data;
- correct inaccurate data;
- request erasure;
- restrict processing;
- object to processing;
- receive portable data where applicable;
- withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis.

You have an unconditional right to object at any time to processing of your personal data for direct marketing.

Requests may be sent to webify.reception@gmail.com.

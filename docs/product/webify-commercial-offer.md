# Webify commercial offer — canonical operating definition

Status: **INTERNAL CANONICAL OFFER — production pricing and customer legal approval remain gated**

## Commercial identity

Customers buy a service from **Webify Digital Solutions Ltd**. VERIDRA is the internal assessment, evidence and operating platform; it is not the customer-facing SaaS product.

## 1. Initial Digital Presence Assessment & Improvement

This is the default first engagement.

### Outcome

Establish a bounded evidence-based baseline of the agreed public website/digital presence, identify material issues, complete only the remediation explicitly included in the accepted proposal, verify the resulting state, and hand over a customer-readable report.

### Base deliverables

- baseline assessment and evidence;
- prioritised findings and affected-page evidence where supported;
- agreed bounded remediation;
- verification/re-assessment of completed remediation;
- customer-facing final report;
- delivery, review and handoff record.

### Base exclusions

Unless explicitly added through the accepted proposal/change control, the engagement excludes unlimited remediation, platform replacement, unrestricted copywriting/redesign, paid media, penetration testing, legal/accessibility/security certification, third-party fees and unrelated development.

### Customer responsibilities

The customer must control or be authorised to change the target, provide approved access when required, maintain necessary third-party subscriptions/services, avoid sending passwords/MFA/card data through ordinary VERIDRA records, and approve material scope changes before additional work starts.

### Commercial terms

Price, currency, validity and exact delivery timeline are confirmed in the proposal. No repository document may be treated as production-approved pricing until Webify explicitly approves that pricing for sale.

## 2. Presence Care — conditional recurring service

Presence Care is **not an automatic add-on and not the default outcome for every customer**. It may be proposed only when the deal records evidence that ongoing change, regression risk, monitoring or bounded maintenance creates credible recurring value.

### Base recurring outcome

Maintain visibility of the agreed digital-presence scope through scheduled monitoring/re-assessment, review changes/regressions, report progress, and provide the bounded maintenance/remediation allowance stated in the accepted recurring-service version.

### Required contract-specific fields

Before activation the accepted recurring-service version must define:

- monitored scope;
- monitoring/report cadence;
- included work allowance and deliverables;
- response/escalation expectations;
- exclusions and out-of-scope quotation rule;
- fee, currency and billing cadence;
- effective/start date and applicable renewal/cancellation terms.

Work beyond the accepted allowance is separately approved/quoted.

## Pricing status

The historical Ireland €149 activation + €99/month, UK £129 + £89/month and US $179 + $119/month values are **test-market / Stripe-sandbox reference scenarios, not universal production-approved prices**. The Ireland €149 + €99/month scenario remains the canonical M3 Stripe sandbox test so provider evidence is reproducible; it does not force the same commercial structure on every real customer.

## Proposal rule

Discovery may narrow or adapt the canonical wording, but must not silently remove the service boundary. Recurring pricing may appear only after evidence-backed Presence Care qualification. Price remains operator-confirmed until a later explicit production-pricing decision replaces this gate.

## Change control

Anything materially outside the accepted scope, deliverables, assumptions or recurring allowance must use the existing change-request/additional-work path before work starts.

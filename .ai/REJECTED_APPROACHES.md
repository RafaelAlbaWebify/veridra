# Rejected / Abandoned Approaches

## RA-001 — Treat synthetic E2E completion as permission for real outreach
Rejected because CI/local synthetic flows do not prove deployment, SMTP, Stripe, accounting, legal approval or operator execution on real infrastructure.
Evidence: #284 reopened and #296 created after review.
Reconsideration: never; real-prospect gate must remain objective.

## RA-002 — Build a full Semrush-style proprietary market-data platform
Rejected because VERIDRA is an evidence/agency workflow product, not a keyword/backlink/traffic-estimation database.
Reconsideration: only via explicitly licensed external data integrations with business case.

## RA-003 — Active penetration testing / exploitation as assessment scope
Rejected for product/safety boundary. Passive public posture only.
Reconsideration: separate product/service with appropriate authorization and controls, not Presence Care default.

## RA-004 — Infer AI visibility from robots/structured data/readiness alone
Rejected because technical readiness/crawler policy are not proof of actual provider visibility.
Reconsideration: sampled provider evidence may be added but must be labeled sampled.

## RA-005 — Store customer passwords or sensitive secrets in ordinary forms/docs
Rejected for security/privacy reasons.
Reconsideration: no; use secure delegated/password-manager mechanisms.

## RA-006 — Make VERIDRA the accounting ledger
Rejected because payment/tax/invoice truth belongs to external provider/accounting evidence.
Reconsideration: only after a separately designed accounting subsystem with tax/accounting review.

## RA-007 — Premature multi-writer/distributed persistence redesign
Rejected for first-customer stage because current single-writer model is sufficient if deployment respects the constraint.
Reconsideration: when real scale/concurrency demands it.

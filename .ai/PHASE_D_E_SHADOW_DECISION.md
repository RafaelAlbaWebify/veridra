## What the shadow set proves

### 1. Activation/remediation value is real but heterogeneous
Some businesses have obvious one-off remediation work. Others do not.

Presence Care must not manufacture defects to justify a sale.

### 2. Recurring value is strongest where independent public surfaces can drift
The strongest recurring case is not generic “website maintenance”. It is monitoring of:
- website facts and regressions;
- dated/stale content;
- primary CTAs and booking reachability;
- Google Business Profile consistency when supported;
- external booking/chat/conversion dependencies;
- public links/social/contact journeys.

### 3. Healthy businesses are the pricing stress test
Dublin Orthodontist demonstrates that a professionally managed practice may have little monthly corrective work.

A fixed €99/month offer to every dental practice is therefore **not yet supported as a universal default**.

### 4. Operator-time evidence
Human guided review on the validated sample:
- median usable review time: 15 minutes;
- average usable review time: 13.6 minutes;
- the intended recurring allowance is 30 minutes/month.

This suggests the monitoring/review component can fit inside the allowance **when VERIDRA prefilters evidence and the operator does not perform open-ended competitor/provider research**.

However:
- report finalisation/customer communication time has not yet been directly timed in a real delivery;
- remediation work itself must stay outside the monthly allowance when it exceeds the bounded scope;
- external-provider/GBP checks may add time and require reliable acquisition support.

## Recurring-value decision

### Outcome: CONDITIONAL

The evidence supports a recurring Presence Care service, but **not a universal €99/month subscription for every SMB regardless of need**.

### Supported positioning
€99/month is plausible when the customer has at least one meaningful recurring-risk surface, for example:
- multiple public business-information surfaces;
- Google Business Profile/local-search dependency;
- booking platform;
- chatbot/CRM/public conversion tooling;
- frequently changing offers/events/content;
- prior evidence of drift/regression;
- owner explicitly values low-noise monitoring/reporting.

### Weak-fit profile
For businesses with:
- professionally managed website;
- stable public facts;
- no material drift;
- no frequently changing content;
- mature external conversion tooling already actively maintained;

monthly €99 may deliver too little visible value.

### Commercial implication
Presence Care should be **qualified**, not indiscriminately sold.

A practical initial policy:
1. activation/remediation is priced separately when needed;
2. €99/month Presence Care is offered only after a qualification gate confirms credible recurring surfaces/value;
3. low-fit businesses receive either:
   - no recurring offer;
   - a lighter/less frequent monitoring option;
   - or a one-off audit/remediation engagement.

Do not invent a lower-priced tier until real prospect/customer evidence justifies exact pricing/cadence.

## 30-minute monthly allowance policy

Inside allowance:
- bounded VERIDRA rerun/review;
- regression/stale-content review;
- public CTA/link/booking reachability;
- public NAP/hours consistency;
- concise report;
- very small content/config corrections only when pre-authorised and safely within scope.

Outside allowance / separate quote:
- redesign;
- major copy/content work;
- CRM/PMS/chatbot implementation;
- booking-system configuration;
- substantial SEO;
- complex cross-provider reconciliation;
- anything involving patient/clinical data or authenticated customer systems without explicit scoped access.

## Product gaps surfaced repeatedly

Do not implement automatically. Aggregate as post-#297 candidates.

High-value candidates:
1. conversion journey / CTA destination integrity;
2. stale dated offers/events;
3. social/external-link reachability;
4. website ↔ Google Business Profile ↔ booking consistency;
5. booking/chat/conversion-provider detection;
6. source/code + AI interpretation for visual/template/maintenance debt;
7. creator/provider context and competitive intelligence.

These should be prioritized by frequency + value + automability, not novelty.
